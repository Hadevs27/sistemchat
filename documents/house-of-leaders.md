## 1. Overview

**House of Leaders (HOL)** presents itself as:

> “A global stage for leadership. Rooted in Indonesia. Built for the world.”

HOL is positioned around leadership and is intended for people who already lead and carry real decisions and responsibilities.

The official website describes HOL as being built for serious leaders rather than primarily for people who aspire to feel like leaders.

HOL describes its purpose as building the stage and environment that serious leaders have been missing.

## 2. How House of Leaders Started

House of Leaders was founded in **2015**.

The initiative began when **Brilliant Liu** was learning about leadership from his mentor.

He started sharing what he was learning through his personal Instagram account because he believed the lessons were too valuable to keep to himself.

As the audience grew, the initiative developed beyond a personal social media account and became a separate platform called **House of Leaders**.

The founding story is therefore associated with a decision to share leadership lessons and create a dedicated platform around them.

## 3. Growth of House of Leaders

According to the official House of Leaders website, the platform grew to **1.5 million followers**.

The website describes this growth as part of a broader journey that taught Brilliant Liu about leadership and the power of personal brand.

The public website currently highlights:

- Founded in 2015
- 1.5M followers

## 4. Founder

**Brilliant Liu** is the founder of **House of Leaders** and the **HOL Group**.

The official website states that he started House of Leaders in 2015.

His public story also includes building his personal brand seriously beginning in **2019**.

By **2020**, he was teaching and consulting others on personal branding.

The website describes his work and the development of HOL as connected to the belief that what people learn can be valuable when shared with others.

## 5. What House of Leaders Is

House of Leaders is positioned as a platform and stage for established leaders.

The official website distinguishes HOL from leadership content designed primarily for people who want to feel like leaders.

HOL is instead described as being for people who are already leading and carrying meaningful decisions and responsibilities.

The platform emphasizes:

- leadership;
- a strong point of view;
- meaningful peer environments;
- opportunities for serious leaders to connect and develop.

## 6. What House of Leaders Is Building

The current public website identifies three major areas:

1. **Media**
2. **Community**
3. **Events**

These areas form part of the next chapter of the House of Leaders ecosystem.

## 7. Media

The Media initiative focuses on leadership content with a genuine point of view.

The website emphasizes that the content is intended to be more substantial than generic motivational quote content.

The public description positions the Media platform as a place for serious leaders to follow leadership-oriented editorial content.

The website associates Media with an **editorial** direction.

## 8. Community

The Community initiative centers around **HOL Inner Circle**.

The website describes HOL Inner Circle as a room/community for serious leaders who want to connect with peers who understand the realities and costs of leadership.

The public website currently indicates that the Inner Circle is **coming** / being built.

The website describes the initiative as being developed deliberately rather than being rushed.

## 9. Events

The Events initiative is focused on bringing **world-class names to Indonesian stages**.

The official website describes the Events direction as creating an in-person environment for leadership.

The public positioning connects Events with the broader House of Leaders goal of building a stage for serious leaders.

## 10. HOL Inner Circle

**HOL Inner Circle** is a community initiative under House of Leaders.

Its stated purpose is to create a room for serious leaders to connect with other leaders.

The website currently presents the Inner Circle as **coming**.

The public information does not specify a final launch date, membership price, member count, or complete membership terms.

Therefore the Specialist must not invent such details.

## 11. Brand N Purpose

**Brand N Purpose (BNP)** is a **personal-brand incubator under the HOL Group**.

It was created for established business owners and leaders who have already built something real but whose personal brand has not yet caught up with the level at which they operate.

The website describes the purpose as building the person who builds the brand, rather than simply building a brand for the person.

## 12. Brand N Purpose Launch

Brand N Purpose became its own company under the HOL Group in **2025**.

The public website connects this development with Brilliant Liu's earlier personal-brand work.

The timeline presented publicly is:

- **2019:** Brilliant Liu began building his personal brand seriously.
- **2020:** He began teaching and consulting others about personal branding.
- **2025:** This work became Brand N Purpose under the HOL Group.

## 13. Brand N Purpose Audience

BNP is designed for:

- established business owners;
- established leaders;
- people who have already built something meaningful;
- people whose personal brand does not yet reflect the level at which they operate.

It is not described as a beginner-only personal-brand course.

## 14. Brand N Purpose Focus

The public website lists the following areas:

- **Personal Brand**
- **Strategy**
- **Positioning**
- **Content**
- **Execution**

The program is presented as an incubator environment intended to build the person's ability to build their own brand.

## 15. Connection Between HOL and Brand N Purpose

Brand N Purpose sits under the **HOL Group**.

Its development is connected to the personal-brand expertise that Brilliant Liu developed while building his own personal brand and teaching others.

The public site presents BNP as part of the wider ecosystem around House of Leaders.

## 16. Current Direction

The official website describes the next chapter of House of Leaders as a **global media and events brand**.

At the same time, the organization is continuing to build its:

- Media;
- Community;
- Events ecosystem.

The website also highlights the development of the HOL Inner Circle.
